# ECON1626 Labour Policy Proposal

This repository contains an evidence-based policy proposal on artificial intelligence and the Australian labour market.

## Repository guide

- [proposal.md](proposal.md) — the 1,200-word policy brief.
- [reflection.md](reflection.md) — the separate 200-word AI-use reflection.

The brief advises the Australian Department of Employment and Workplace Relations on implementing the workforce commitments in the National AI Plan. It evaluates three costed policy options and recommends a three-year AI Workforce Transition Package.

Sources use RMIT Harvard referencing style and link to the original publications.
